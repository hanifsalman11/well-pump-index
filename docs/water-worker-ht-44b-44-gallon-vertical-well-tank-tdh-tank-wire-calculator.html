Frequently Asked Questions (Water Worker HT-44B (44 Gallon) Vertical Well Tank)

How do I calculate Total Dynamic Head (TDH) for Water Worker HT-44B (44 Gallon) Vertical Well Tank?

Add your pumping water depth (180 ft), vertical elevation to the pressure tank, pipe friction loss (~13.8 ft), and household operating pressure multiplied by 2.31 (50 PSI × 2.31 = 115.5 ft). That gives a Total Dynamic Head of 309 feet.

What size pressure tank do I need for a 12 GPM well pump?

To guarantee a minimum 1-minute pump run time (12 gallons of usable drawdown), divide 12 GPM by the 40/60 PSI drawdown factor (0.27). That requires at least a 44-gallon total volume diaphragm tank providing 11.9 gallons of drawdown.

What air pressure should be in my well tank for a 40/60 PSI switch?

With all water drained from the tank and zero water pressure on the gauge, inflate the top Schrader air valve to 38 PSI (2 PSI below the 40 PSI cut-in pressure).
